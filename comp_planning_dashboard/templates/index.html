Governance & data-quality flags
  • Performance is one of several inputs — priority and raise size also depend on range position, tenure, criticality, and equity, so a high performance score alone does not guarantee a large raise.
  • Pay-equity concerns (manual flag or computed peer-group gap) are surfaced for HR/compensation review. This tool does not make a legal pay-equity determination.
  • Protected characteristics (age, race, gender, disability, etc.) are not collected or used anywhere in this model.
  • Supervisor ratings and objective performance-metric scores are kept in separate columns throughout — they are only ever blended into a labeled "combined score," never silently merged.
  • Any HR override requires a written justification, which is stored alongside the system recommendation for audit.
